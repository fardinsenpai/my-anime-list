Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Fardin's Anime Hub ("we", "us", "the site") is a fan-made anime listing and chat website. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep it as simple and as honest as we can.

In one line

We only collect what is needed to run the site and to personalise the chat. We never sell your information, and we never see your passwords.

1. Information we handle

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in is optional — you can use the site without it. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares the following with us:

  • your name (as shown on your Google account)
  • your email address
  • your profile picture link (if your account has one)
  • a unique Google account ID that identifies your account to us

We use this only to show you as signed in, to display your picture, and to let the site's AI assistant address you by name. We do not receive or store your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, files, calendar, or any other Google data.

Information you type or upload

  • Chat messages to the AI assistant. They are kept in your own browser, and they are sent to our AI provider (Groq) so it can generate a reply.
  • Public contributions — for example comments in Hot Takes, or entries in the Hall of Champions. These are stored in our database (Supabase) and are visible to other visitors.
  • Preferences — your avatar choices, chat history, and similar settings are stored locally in your own browser.

Technical and usage data

Like most websites, the services that host and power this site process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) so they can deliver pages and keep the site secure. We also use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (for example, which pages are visited, approximate region, and device type). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. We use this to improve the site — not to build advertising profiles.

2. How we use this information

  • to operate the site and its features;
  • to personalise the chat assistant (for example, using your first name);
  • to show your contributions to other visitors;
  • to keep the service safe, working, and free of abuse;
  • to understand overall usage and improve the site.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or re-marketing.
  • We do not read anything beyond what is described on this page.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make the site work. Each of them has its own privacy policy:

  • Google — sign-in (optional) and website analytics
  • Supabase — accounts and database storage
  • Groq — generating the AI assistant's replies
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • AniList — anime titles and character artwork
  • jsDelivr — delivering an open-source code library

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the site and its users from abuse.

5. Storage and security

Account and contribution data is stored with Supabase. We take reasonable measures to protect it, and access to the site's admin tools is restricted. Please note that no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure. Chat history and avatar preferences stay in your own browser and disappear if you clear your browser data.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices, and deleting your data

  • You can use the site without signing in at any time.
  • You can sign out whenever you like.
  • You can clear your local data by clearing your browser storage for this site.
  • To delete your account data, or to ask what we hold about you, just contact us below. We will act on reasonable requests.

8. Anime content and copyright

Anime titles, artwork, and character images belong to their respective creators, studios, and rights holders, and are shown here for informational purposes. Anime and character information is provided from the AniList database. This is a personal fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any studio, publisher, or streaming service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. When we do, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change.
